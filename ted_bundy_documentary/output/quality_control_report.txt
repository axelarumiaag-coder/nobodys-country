3. HISTORICAL VERIFICATION
  Script edits made for accuracy (see ted_bundy_script.txt):
   - 'Days later ... Pensacola' -> 'Weeks later': Chi Omega attack 15 Jan 1978; Pensacola arrest 15 Feb 1978.
   - 'convicted for the Florida attacks' (1979) -> 'convicted of the sorority house murders': the Miami trial (verdict July 1979)
     concerned Chi Omega. The Kimberly Leach conviction came in 1980 and is not mentioned in the narration.
   - 'well-dressed man' -> 'polite, clean-cut man', closer to the reported 1974 witness descriptions.
  On-screen dates and places used (well documented in the standard record; recheck before publication):
   - born 24 Nov 1946; B.A. psychology, University of Washington, 1972
   - 1974 cases in Washington and Oregon; Lake Sammamish, July 1974: witnesses described a man ('Ted') with his arm in a sling asking women for help
   - fall 1974: moved to Salt Lake City for law school (University of Utah)
   - 16 Aug 1975: traffic stop and arrest in Granger, Utah; ski mask, crowbar, handcuffs and rope reported found in the car
   - 1976: aggravated kidnapping conviction (Utah)
   - 7 Jun 1977: escape from the Pitkin County Courthouse, Aspen
   - 30 Dec 1977: escape from the Garfield County Jail, Glenwood Springs
   - 15 Jan 1978: Chi Omega, Florida State University, Tallahassee; Lisa Levy and Margaret Bowman killed
   - 15 Feb 1978: arrested in Pensacola
   - 1979: Miami trial, televised nationwide; he took part in his own defence; convicted July 1979; sentenced to death 31 Jul 1979
   - 24 Jan 1989: executed at Florida State Prison, near Starke
  Points of uncertainty:
   - 'Televised nationwide' is the commonly reported description of the 1979 trial coverage.
   - The colour on the 1974-1975 evidence board (WA/OR/UT/CO) shows the states where cases were later attributed to him. It does not show what investigators knew at the time.
   - The route lines on the maps are simplified and labelled as such.
  No quotations, witness statements, headlines or documents were invented. Case-file cards are labelled as editorial graphics.
