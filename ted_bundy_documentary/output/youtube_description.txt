TITLE (suggested, under 70 characters):
Ted Bundy: The Perfect Mask | The Life and Crimes of a Serial Killer

---------------------------------------------------------------------

He was polite, intelligent and completely ordinary. He studied law, worked in politics and volunteered on a crisis hotline. And for years, he hid a terrifying secret.

This documentary follows the case of Ted Bundy in chronological order: his childhood built on family secrets, the disappearances that began in Washington State in 1974, the survivor who helped identify him, his two escapes from custody in Colorado, the attacks in Florida, the trials that captivated America, his confessions on death row, and his execution in 1989.

The film is built from archival photographs, maps, case dates and verified historical sources. It keeps the victims at the center of the story, not the man who took their lives.
