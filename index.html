Nobody's Country Uploader

Nobody's Country Uploader is a private, single-user application that publishes videos to the YouTube channel Nobody's Country and reads that channel's own analytics.

It is not a public service. It is not distributed, sold, or made available to other users. It is operated solely by the channel's owner, on the channel's own account.

What it does

Uploads video files and their metadata to the owner's YouTube channel, and retrieves performance statistics for that same channel.
